What is ICT?

Information and Communications Technology (ICT) is the infrastructure and tools that let people create, process, and share information. It covers devices, software, networks, communications, cloud services, and the policies that keep those systems secure.

ICT and IT

Information Technology (IT) usually focuses on computers: software, hardware, and networking. ICT is broader. It includes those IT building blocks plus telecommunications and other digital systems that people use to communicate.

That range runs from familiar tools such as phones through to newer fields such as artificial intelligence and robotics.

Where it shows up

ICT underpins business, education, medicine, and entertainment. When those sectors work well, it is often because the underlying systems can store, protect, and move information reliably.

That is the stack I work in day to day: networks, data, reporting, and the apps that make those systems usable.
